ShopKart Plus membership

Last reviewed: 1 Jul 2026.

ShopKart Plus costs 999 rupees a year. Members get:

  • no delivery fee on any order (large-furniture assembly fees still apply);
  • early access to sales, 24 hours before everyone else;
  • priority support.

You can cancel within 7 days of joining for a full refund. After 7 days the fee is not refundable.
