# Damaged or defective items

Owner: Customer Support. Last reviewed: 2 Sep 2026.

Customers report damage from the order page within 48 hours of delivery, with at least one photo. Large
furniture damage can be reported within 7 days, because boxes are often opened later.

## What happens next
1. Support checks the photo. Clear damage is approved on the spot; unclear cases go to the warehouse team.
2. We book a pickup. Groceries and personal-care items are **not** picked up: we refund them without a return.
3. Refunds for damaged items are issued within five working days of the return reaching our warehouse. For
   items we don't pick up, the refund is issued within two working days of the approval.
4. The customer may choose a replacement instead of a refund if the item is in stock.

The refund goes back to the original payment method, with the exceptions in
[Returns and refunds](returns-and-refunds.md): cash on delivery and voucher payments follow their own pages.

## Escalation
If a pickup is missed twice, Support escalates to Logistics using the
[courier delay runbook](../runbooks/courier-delay.md).
