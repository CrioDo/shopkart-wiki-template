# Cancellations

Owner: Customer Support. Last reviewed: 2 Sep 2026.

Customers can cancel any item before it ships, from the Orders page, with a full refund. After it ships,
cancellation isn't possible: the customer refuses the delivery (COD) or returns it under
[Returns and refunds](returns-and-refunds.md).

Seller-cancelled orders (out of stock) are refunded automatically, and the customer gets a 100-rupee
apology voucher if the order was placed during a sale (see [Festive sale 2026](../faq/festive-sale-2026.md)).
