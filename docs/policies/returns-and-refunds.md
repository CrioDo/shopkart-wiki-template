# Returns and refunds

Owner: Customer Support (see [Customer Support team](../teams/customer-support.md)). Last reviewed: 2 Sep 2026.

## Return windows
| Category | Window | How |
|---|---|---|
| Fashion, footwear, accessories | 30 days from delivery | drop-off at a partner store or doorstep pickup |
| Electronics and appliances | 10 days from delivery | doorstep pickup only, with the original box |
| Large furniture (sofas, beds, wardrobes) | 10 days from delivery | doorstep pickup booked by Logistics; the item must be unassembled |
| Groceries and personal care | not returnable, unless damaged | see [Damaged items](damaged-items.md) |

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued when the return reaches our warehouse and passes a check, usually within 2 working days of
arrival. The money then reaches the customer:
- card and UPI payments: 3-5 working days, depending on the bank;
- ShopKart Wallet: instantly;
- cash on delivery orders: see [Cash on delivery](cash-on-delivery.md), because we need a bank account;
- orders paid fully or partly with a voucher or gift card: see [Vouchers and gift cards](vouchers-and-gift-cards.md).

## What we never refund
Delivery fees on change-of-mind returns, and ShopKart Plus membership fees after the first 7 days (see the
[Plus membership page](../help-centre/plus-membership.html)).
