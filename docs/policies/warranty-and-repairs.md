# Warranty and repairs

Owner: Customer Support. Last reviewed: 2 Sep 2026.

Electronics carry the brand's warranty, which the brand's service centre handles. ShopKart's own-brand items
(the "Kart Basics" range) carry a 1-year ShopKart warranty: we repair or replace the item, and if neither is
possible, refund it as a damaged item (see [Damaged items](damaged-items.md)).

Extended warranty plans can be bought within 30 days of delivery and are not refundable after 15 days.
