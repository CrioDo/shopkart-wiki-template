# Shipping and delivery

Owner: Logistics (see [Logistics team](../teams/logistics.md)). Last reviewed: 1 Oct 2026.

## Delivery fees
Orders over 2,000 rupees ship free; smaller orders pay a flat 49-rupee delivery fee. ShopKart Plus members pay
no delivery fee on any order (see the [Plus membership page](../help-centre/plus-membership.html)). Large
furniture always has a 499-rupee assembly-and-delivery fee, which Plus does not cover.

## Delivery times
| Destination | Standard | Express (99 rupees, metros only) |
|---|---|---|
| Metro cities | 2-3 days | next day if ordered before 2 pm |
| Other cities | 3-5 days | not offered |
| Remote PIN codes | 5-8 days | not offered |

## Changing the address
You can change your delivery address until the order is packed, from the Orders page. After that, Support can
only reroute through the courier, which may add a day.
