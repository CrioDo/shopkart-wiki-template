# Bulk and business orders

Owner: Payments. Last reviewed: 1 Oct 2026. The signed policy is in the PDF
[Bulk orders policy](bulk-orders-policy.pdf).

Orders of more than 20 units of one item, or over 2 lakh rupees, are business orders: they need a GST number,
are paid by bank transfer or card only (no COD), and can't use promotional vouchers.
