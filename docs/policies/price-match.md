# Price match

Owner: Catalog (see [Catalog team](../teams/catalog.md)). Last reviewed: 20 Jun 2026.

If the price of an item drops within 7 days of the order, the customer can claim the difference as ShopKart
Wallet credit, once per order. Sale prices during the [festive sale](../faq/festive-sale-2026.md) are excluded.
We don't match other shops' prices.
