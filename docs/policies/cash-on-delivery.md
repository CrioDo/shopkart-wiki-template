# Cash on delivery (COD)

Owner: Payments. Last reviewed: 15 Jul 2026.

Cash on delivery is available for orders under 10,000 rupees, at serviceable PIN codes only. Large furniture
can't be paid by COD at any value. Customers may pay the courier in cash or by scanning the courier's UPI code.

## Refunds on COD orders
We have no card or account to send the money back to, so the customer adds a bank account (or UPI ID) on the
order page. Once the refund is issued, the bank transfer adds about 2 working days. A customer may instead
choose ShopKart Wallet credit, which is instant.

## Limits
A customer with three refused COD deliveries in 90 days loses COD for 6 months (see
[Fraud review queue](../runbooks/fraud-review-queue.md)).
