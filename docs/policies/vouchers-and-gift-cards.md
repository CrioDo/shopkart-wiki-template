# Vouchers and gift cards

Owner: Payments (see [Payments team](../teams/payments.md)). Last reviewed: 12 Aug 2026.

## Kinds
- **Promotional vouchers** (for example `FESTIVE500`): a fixed discount; no cash value.
- **Gift cards**: bought with money, valid for 12 months, can be combined with any payment method.
- **Refund vouchers**: issued when a voucher-paid order is returned.

## Refunds on orders paid with a voucher or gift card
- The part paid by a **gift card** goes back to the same gift card, and its expiry moves 30 days later if it would
  otherwise expire within 30 days.
- The part paid by a **promotional voucher** is never refunded as cash. If the return is within the voucher's
  validity, the voucher is reinstated; if it has expired, the customer gets a refund voucher for the same amount,
  valid for 30 days.
- The rest (card, UPI, wallet, cash on delivery) follows [Returns and refunds](returns-and-refunds.md).

Vouchers can't be used on ShopKart Plus membership or on gift cards themselves.
