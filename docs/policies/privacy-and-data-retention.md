# Privacy and data retention

Owner: Trust and Safety (see [Trust and Safety team](../teams/trust-and-safety.md)). Last reviewed: 5 Aug 2026.

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Order history | 8 years (tax law) |
| Support chats and call recordings | 18 months |
| Payment tokens | until the customer removes the card |
| Delivery photos taken by couriers | 90 days |

Support agents never ask for a full card number, CVV or OTP. Customers can download their data from the
account page; deletion is covered in [Account closure](account-closure.md).
