# On-call escalation

Last reviewed: 12 Oct 2026.

Owner: Platform. Every team has a primary and a secondary on-call engineer, paged through PagerDuty.

| When | Who to page |
|---|---|
| Working hours (09:00-19:00 IST) | the team's primary on-call |
| 19:00-22:00 IST | the primary on-call; secondary after 15 minutes without an answer |
| 22:00-09:00 IST | the Platform duty manager first, who pages the right team |

SEV1 incidents page the Platform duty manager at any hour (see
[Incident severity levels](../engineering/incident-severity-levels.md)).
