# Runbook: fraud review queue

Last reviewed: 4 Jul 2026.

Owner: Trust and Safety. Orders flagged by the risk model wait in the review queue. Reviewers clear them within 4
hours during the day. Customers with three refused COD deliveries in 90 days lose COD for 6 months (see
[Cash on delivery](../policies/cash-on-delivery.md)); reviewers can lift the block once, with a note.
