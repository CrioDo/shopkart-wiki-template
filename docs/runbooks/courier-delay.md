# Runbook: courier delay

Last reviewed: 30 Sep 2026.

Owner: Logistics on-call. Triggered when a courier misses more than 5% of pickups or deliveries in a region for a
day, or when Support escalates a missed pickup twice (see [Damaged items](../policies/damaged-items.md)).
1. Confirm in the courier performance report.
2. Move new orders in the region to the second-choice courier.
3. For customers with a missed pickup, book a priority pickup and add a 50-rupee wallet credit.
