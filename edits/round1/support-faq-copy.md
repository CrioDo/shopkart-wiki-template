# Support FAQ (internal, copy)

Last reviewed: 15 Oct 2026.

**A customer wants to return a sofa bought with a festive voucher. What do I tell them?** Large furniture has a
10-day window and a Logistics pickup; the voucher part follows the voucher rules. Check both pages before
answering.

**Can a customer change the address after packing?** Only through a courier reroute; see
[Shipping and delivery](../policies/shipping-and-delivery.md).

**Can I promise a refund date?** Quote the policy's range, never a specific date.
