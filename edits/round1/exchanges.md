# Exchanges

Owner: Customer Support. Last reviewed: 3 Nov 2026 (new page).

Fashion and footwear can be exchanged for another size or colour of the same item within 30 days of delivery,
once per order. The courier collects the old item when it delivers the new one, so there is no refund and no
second delivery fee. Electronics, furniture and sale items can't be exchanged: return them instead (see
[Returns and refunds](returns-and-refunds.md)).
